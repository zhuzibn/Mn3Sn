Energy, fields and provenance

The ordered-pair energy is E = (J/2) Σ Aᵢⱼ mᵢ·mⱼ − (D/2) Σ Cᵢⱼ x̂·(mᵢ×mⱼ) − K Σ(eᵢ·mᵢ)². Every bond has Aᵢⱼ = ½ and Cᵢⱼ = ±½, with Aⱼᵢ = Aᵢⱼ and Cⱼᵢ = −Cᵢⱼ. The outer ½ factors remove ordered-pair double counting. Uniform K = 0.196 meV.

Graph data come from MATLAB impulse measurements in prepare_periodic.m, checked against the independently enumerated wrapped stencil in periodic_six_states.py. The source field is Bᵢ = −(J/μ) Σ Aᵢⱼ mⱼ + (D/μ) Σ Cᵢⱼ(0,mⱼz,−mⱼy) + (2K/μ)(eᵢ·mᵢ)eᵢ. The display never supplies geometry to the solver. Refresh instructions and evidence limits are in the production report.
